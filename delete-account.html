Delete your Hand and Foot account

Hand and Foot, by Amberlane Labs

You can ask us to delete your Hand and Foot account and the data linked to it at any time. You don't need to give a reason.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to amberlanelabs@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Hand and Foot account".
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in (your Google or Facebook email), or include your player name and friend code from the Profile screen, so we can find your account.
  3. We will confirm by email and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, picture or avatar, friend code and level
  • Your friends list and friend requests
  • Your online statistics, badges, Daily Deal scores and streaks
  • Your push notification token
  • Your sign-in account in Firebase Authentication

What may be kept for a short time

  • Your name on a current weekly or Daily Deal leaderboard may remain until that board is cleared, within 30 days.
  • Information we are required to keep by law, if any.
Offline games, settings and statistics are stored only on your phone. To remove them, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's settings.
