Privacy Policy: Hand and Foot

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information the Hand and Foot app ("the app") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is made by Amberlane Labs, the developer name of Talha Azhar ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, email amberlanelabs@gmail.com.

In short: you can play offline without an account, and that play stays on your device. If you sign in to play online, we keep only what is needed for online play: your name, picture, friends and game results. We never sell your information.

1. Playing offline without an account

You can play against computer players without signing in. Your saved games, settings, personal statistics, guest coins and Chest items are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them. If you later log in, your guest coins and items are moved into your account once.

2. Information we collect when you sign in

Online play, friends, the Daily Deal leaderboard and the weekly leaderboard need an account. You can sign in with Google or Facebook. When you do, we collect:

InformationWhy we use it
Your name and profile picture from Google or FacebookTo show you to other players at the table, in friends lists and on leaderboards. You can choose a built-in avatar instead of your picture.
Your email address and account ID (handled by Firebase Authentication)To sign you in securely and keep your account separate from others. Your email is not shown to other players.
Friend code, friends list and friend requestsTo let you add friends and invite them to games.
Game results and statistics (games played, won, lost, level, badges, Daily Deal scores and streaks)To show your stats and level and to rank players on leaderboards.
Moves and quick-chat phrases sent during online gamesTo run the game between players. Chat is limited to preset phrases; there is no free-text chat.
Online status (whether you used the app in the last few minutes)To show friends when you are available to play.
Coins, Chest items you own and use, and your daily-reward streakTo keep your coins and items safe in your account on any phone, and to run online table stakes.
Google Play purchase receipts for coin packs (purchase token and product)To check with Google that a coin pack was paid for before adding the coins, and to make sure it is only counted once.
Push notification token for your deviceTo send game invites and friend requests to your device.

We do not collect your contacts, precise location, photos or files.

3. Notifications

With your permission, the app can send game invites and friend requests through Firebase Cloud Messaging, and an optional daily reminder for the Daily Deal. The daily reminder is created on your device and sends no information to us. You can turn notifications off in the app's settings or in your phone's settings.

4. Advertising and purchases

The free version of the app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID and general device and usage information to show and measure ads, as described in Google's advertising policies. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings.

You can pay to remove ads. Purchases are handled by Google Play; we never receive your card or payment details.

5. Service providers we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging): for sign-in, storing account and game data, and notifications.
  • Google Cloud Run: hosts the game server that runs online games.
  • Facebook Login (Meta): only if you choose to sign in with Facebook.
  • Google AdMob and Google Play: for ads and purchases.

These providers process information on our behalf under their own privacy terms. Data is stored on Google's servers, which may be outside your country.

6. How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Other players can see your name or avatar, picture, level, badges, game stats and leaderboard results. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, friends list, statistics and notification token from our servers within 30 days. Your name may remain on past weekly or Daily Deal leaderboards until those boards are cleared, which happens within 30 days.

8. Deleting your account and data

You can ask us to delete your account and all related data at any time. See how to delete your account.

9. Security

Information is sent between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and only signed-in users can access their own account data.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
